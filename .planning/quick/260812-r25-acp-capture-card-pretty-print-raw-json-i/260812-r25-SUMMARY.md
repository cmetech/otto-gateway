## Deviation — latent bug fixed in passing

`acpCaptureUrl()` returned the relative `'api/acp-capture'`, the only relative
URL in `admin.js` (every other endpoint is absolute `/admin/...`). Its comment
claimed mount-agnosticism, but the admin handler is mounted at a fixed `/admin`
on the outer router, so the claim bought nothing — and at `/admin` **without**
a trailing slash the URL resolved to `/api/acp-capture`, returned 404, and
`renderAcpCapture` hid the entire capture panel. The panel only ever appeared
at `/admin/`. Changed to absolute. Without this, the requested Show Messages
button was unreachable at the URL most people type.

## Color choice

`--gw-healthy` / `--gw-failed` were rejected as button fills: they are tuned as
*foreground* colors against the card surface, and against white label text they
fall under 4.5:1 in at least one theme (`--gw-healthy` light `#059669` → 3.6:1).
The two new fills clear 5:1 with `#FFFFFF` and need no light-theme override,
since a filled button paints its own background.

## Known limit (not addressed)

Each frame's `params` is a JSON *string* on the wire, so its contents stay on
one long escaped line inside the otherwise-indented output. Making it readable
would mean changing `CaptureFrame.Params` to `json.RawMessage` — an API shape
change, out of scope here.
